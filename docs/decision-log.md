# Decision log

Changes from PRD v1.0 (`docs/source/labour-ticket-analytics-prd-v1.0.md`) to v1.1 (`docs/prd.md`), made in the design review of 14 October 2026.
Every PRD figure was independently recomputed from the workbook before the review and reconciled exactly; no change below corrects a number.
"Review" means the grilling session; R1/R2/R3 are its rounds.

| # | Change | Reason | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PRD revised in place as v1.1 rather than rewritten or accepted as-is | The analysis and evidence controls are correct; the problems were scope and cost | R1 Q1 |
| 2 | First user is the consultant on their own machine; the Oliver meeting (unscheduled) is the deadline | Fixes who release one serves and why it must be small | R1 Q2, R1 Q6, R3 Q1, R3 Q2 |

| 23 | Times in a Time cell are paired in order into start and end rather than split on separators | The export uses "_" and "/" both between periods ("730am 1030am _ 230pm 430pm") and between start and end ("7/30am _4/30pm") | Consultant, ticket #5 |

| 31 | The questions for Oliver are the evidence report's per-pattern questions, attached to every example of that pattern with "what the answer changes" | The report asks its questions per pattern, not per example | Agent, ticket #7 |
| 32 | E and F carry no review note, and the drawer says the evidence report has none | The report gives no "My review notes" for them; nothing is invented | Agent, ticket #7 |
| 33 | A Review Outcome is keyed by the Snapshot fingerprint and the pair's two Excel rows, lower first, and attaches only to a pair some Candidate links; the two ticket numbers are stored beside it for release-two carry-forward, never as the key | Excel rows are the provenance key and fixed within a fingerprint, while ticket numbers can be blank or repeated in the export; carry-forward must revalidate by ticket number and source values anyway (PRD section 5, local review controls) | Agent, ticket #8 |
| 34 | Kept/replaced links are separate from Review Outcomes: any outcome, or none, can sit beside them; both Tickets must be in one possible duplicate when linked; the reverse of an existing link is refused. Only links name the Tickets an adjusted figure may leave out (`replaced_tickets`). | 35 | A save that changes nothing appends no history; changing a saved outcome, note or reference requires a rationale, while a first save and link changes take an optional one; removing a link is a history row from the link to none | History records decisions, not button presses, and a revision is the moment a reason matters | Agent, ticket #8 |

| 38 | Review priorities show each pair's Review Outcome but tiers and ranks ignore outcomes for now; entry 28's "counterpart counts as unresolved" stands until the flagged-duplicate counterpart rule (ticket #11) reads them | No counterpart relationship exists yet for an outcome to resolve, and an outcome alone must have no numerical effect | Agent, ticket #8 |
